## What Is Reliqra?

Reliqra is an inventory, identification and selling platform for collectors and dealers. It covers **coins, currency, cards, comics, bullion and gems**, so one workspace can hold a whole collection or a whole shop.

**Three pillars:**

1. **Identify and Price.** Scan an item, identify it, and see what it is worth.
2. **Inventory.** Keep one record of everything you own, across every item type.
3. **Sell.** List to your marketplaces and keep sold items in sync.
